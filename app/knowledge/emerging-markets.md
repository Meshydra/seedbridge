# Raising in emerging and frontier markets

## Local evidence matters
Funders outside the founder's country often lack local context. Explain the local problem with local numbers (district, number of households, price in local currency converted to USD) and why the solution fits local infrastructure such as basic phones, SMS, mobile money or intermittent connectivity.

## Diaspora and regional capital
Diaspora angel networks, regional funds and development finance institutions often understand local markets better than global investors. Impact investors and blended-finance programs frequently combine grants with investment.

## Currency and regulation
Investors ask how the company handles currency risk, where it is incorporated, and whether it can receive foreign investment and repay or exit. A clear corporate structure removes friction.

## Showing scale potential
A strong case shows how the model expands from one district to the country and then to similar markets in the region, and what changes at each step.
