# Use of funds and milestones

## Tie money to milestones
Funders want to see what the money achieves, not just what it buys. Group spending into 3-5 buckets (for example product, sales, operations) and attach a milestone to each: "Hire one field officer to onboard 500 farmers by June".

## Runway
State how many months the funding lasts at the planned spending rate, and what the business will look like at the end: next round ready, break-even, or a proven pilot ready for a larger grant.

## Typical warning signs
Large founder salaries at idea stage, most of the budget on marketing before the product works, equipment purchases with no utilisation plan, and no money set aside for measuring results.
